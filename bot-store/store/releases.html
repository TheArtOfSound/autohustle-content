Offline license format

No device tracking or mandatory online validation is built. Edition limits are provisional metadata, not enforced in the monitoring engine.

Verify signed releases

When a trusted operator provides a signed manifest and an independently authenticated release public key, verify the files locally before execution:

node sentinel-release.mjs verify-release release-public.pem signed-release.json .

Signed license verification:

node sentinel-release.mjs verify-license license-public.pem signed-license.json

The toolkit does not automatically download or execute updates. The public manifest currently lists hashes only; it is not signed and cannot establish publisher authenticity on its own.
