What Is Recycle It and How Does It Work?

Recycle It is a specialized recycling service dedicated to helping individuals and businesses responsibly dispose of electronic waste (e-waste) and office equipment. Their mission is to reduce landfill waste and promote the reuse of valuable materials, aligning with global sustainability goals.

The process is straightforward:

  1. Collection: Recycle It offers pickup services or drop-off options, making it easy to dispose of old equipment.
  2. Sorting and Processing: Once collected, items are sorted to determine if they can be refurbished or recycled.
  3. Eco-Friendly Disposal: Items that can’t be reused are broken down into recyclable materials, ensuring minimal environmental impact.

Recycle It ensures that all data-bearing devices are securely wiped or destroyed, protecting your privacy while contributing to a greener planet.

Top 5 Home Office Equipment Items Recycle It Handles

Recycle It specializes in recycling a wide range of home and office equipment. Here are five of the most common items they process:

1. Computers and Laptops

Old computers and laptops often contain hazardous materials like lead, mercury, and cadmium. Recycle It safely dismantles these devices, recovering precious metals like gold, silver, and copper while ensuring harmful components are disposed of responsibly.

2. Printers and Copiers

Printers and copiers are notorious for containing toxic substances and non-biodegradable plastics. Recycle It breaks down these machines, recycling metals and plastics while diverting them from landfills.

3. Monitors and Screens

CRT and LCD monitors contain glass, metals, and hazardous chemicals. Recycle It ensures these materials are extracted and reused or disposed of safely, reducing environmental contamination.

4. Office Phones and VoIP Equipment

Old office phones and VoIP systems often end up in drawers or landfills. Recycle It refurbishes usable equipment and recycles the rest, giving these devices a second life or safely extracting recyclable materials.

5. Furniture and Fixtures

Recycle It also handles office furniture like desks, chairs, and filing cabinets. Items in good condition are donated or resold, while damaged pieces are recycled for materials like metal and wood.

3. Convenience

4. Compliance with Regulations

Recycle It stays up-to-date with local and international e-waste regulations, ensuring that your disposal practices are fully compliant. This is especially crucial for businesses subject to environmental laws.

5. Community Impact

Practical Takeaways

If you’re considering recycling your home or office equipment with Recycle It, here’s what you should keep in mind:

  1. Schedule a Pickup or Drop-Off: Visit Recycle It’s website to book a pickup or find a nearby drop-off location. Their team will guide you through the process.
  2. Prepare Your Equipment: Remove any personal data from devices (though Recycle It handles secure data destruction, it’s good practice to back up files).
  3. Check for Rebates: Some items may qualify for recycling rebates, so ask about potential incentives when you schedule your pickup.
