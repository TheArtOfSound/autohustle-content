Pro Tips for Maximizing Your Beauty & Lifestyle Tools

Extend the Life of Your Jewelry

If you invest in a piece like the 18K stainless steel necklace, keep it looking new by storing it in a jewelry box or anti-tarnish pouch. Avoid exposing it to harsh chemicals, such as perfumes or lotions, which can dull the finish over time.

Keep Your Power Bank Efficient

To prolong the life of your portable charger, avoid overcharging it and store it in a cool, dry place. Regularly clean the charging ports to prevent dust buildup, which can affect performance.

Maintain Your Bug Zapper

For best results, place your UV bug zapper in areas where pests are most active, like near windows or outdoor seating. Clean the light grid periodically to remove dead insects and ensure maximum effectiveness.
