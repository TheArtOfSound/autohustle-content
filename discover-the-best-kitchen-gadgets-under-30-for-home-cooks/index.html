Buying Guide: How to Choose the Best Kitchen Gadgets Under $30

Not sure which gadget is right for you? Here are a few key factors to consider:

  1. Material and Durability

Look for tools made from high-quality materials like stainless steel, silicone, or stoneware. Avoid flimsy plastics that may break or wear out quickly.

  1. Ease of Cleaning

Dishwasher-safe gadgets save time and effort. Check reviews to ensure the product holds up after multiple washes.

  1. Versatility

If you’re tight on space, opt for gadgets that serve multiple purposes, like the Microplane or the Le Creuset baker.

  1. Ergonomics

Comfortable handles and non-slip grips make cooking more enjoyable. Test the tool’s weight and balance before purchasing.
