Template 7: Sales Script for First Customer Calls

When to use: When reaching out to potential customers for the first time to pitch your product/service.

Sales Script for [Product/Service Name]

Opening (15-30 seconds):

“Hi [First Name], this is [Your Name] from [Your Business]. I noticed [specific detail about their business/pain point], and I think we can help. Do you have 5 minutes to chat?”

Introducing Your Solution (30-60 seconds):

“We specialize in [briefly explain what you do]. For businesses like yours, we’ve helped [specific result, e.g., ‘reduce costs by 20%’ or ‘increase sales by 30%’]. Does that sound like something you’d be interested in?”

Handling Objections (Examples):

  • Objection: “We’re already using [Competitor].”

Response: “I understand. Many of our clients started with [Competitor] too. The key difference is [your unique value]. For example, [give a brief case study or testimonial].”

  • Objection: “It’s too expensive.”

Response: “I get that. Let me ask—what’s your current solution costing you in [time/money/resources]? Our solution is designed to [solve specific pain point], which often offsets the cost.”

  • Objection: “I’m not sure this is the right time.”

Response: “I completely understand. When would be a better time to follow up? [Set a date].”

Closing (30 seconds):

“Based on what we’ve discussed, would you be open to [next step, e.g., ‘a free trial,’ ‘a demo,’ or ‘a proposal’]? If so, I can send over the details right now. Does that work for you?”

Follow-Up:

“Great! I’ll follow up with [next step] by [date]. Thanks for your time, [First Name]!”
