40 Remote Work Setup Checklist Items

Updated: April 01, 2026

40-item checklist for setting up a remote workstation — includes hardware, software, and ergonomic checks.

What's Inside

  • 40 essential checklist items
  • covers hardware, software, and ergonomics
  • step-by-step verification
  • [ ] Item 1: Measure your desk height so your elbows rest at 90 degrees when typing
  • [ ] Item 2: Position your monitor directly in front of you at arm’s length
  • [ ] Item 3: Adjust your chair so your feet rest flat on the floor or a footrest
  • [ ] Item 4: Set your monitor height so the top third of the screen is at eye level
  • [ ] Item 5: Keep frequently used items (mouse, notebook, phone) within arm’s reach
  • [ ] Item 6: Use a lumbar support cushion or rolled towel for lower back support
  • [ ] Item 7: Ensure your wrists are straight and not bent while typing
  • [ ] Item 8: Choose an ergonomic chair with adjustable armrests
  • [ ] Item 9: Place your keyboard and mouse at the same height to avoid shoulder strain
  • [ ] Item 10: Use a monitor stand or stack sturdy books to elevate your screen if needed
  • [ ] Item 11: Plug in a wired keyboard and mouse for reliable performance
  • [ ] Item 12: Set up a second monitor for increased productivity and multitasking
  • [ ] Item 13: Use a USB hub to expand ports for peripherals and accessories
  • [ ] Item 14: Install a quality webcam (minimum 1080p) for clear video calls
  • [ ] Item 15: Equip your workspace with a noise-canceling headset for calls
  • [ ] Item 16: Use an external microphone if your headset or webcam mic isn’t clear
  • [ ] Item 17: Connect a power strip with surge protection for all your devices
  • [ ] Item 18: Set up a docking station if using a laptop as your primary device
  • [ ] Item 19: Install a wired or high-quality wireless mouse for precision
  • [ ] Item 20: Use a wired or ergonomic vertical mouse to reduce wrist strain
  • [ ] Item 21: Position your desk near a power outlet to avoid cord clutter
  • [ ] Item 22: Use cable organizers or clips to keep cords neat and tidy
  • [ ] Item 23: Choose a desk with built-in cable management channels
  • [ ] Item 24: Set up a dedicated trash bin or recycling container nearby
  • [ ] Item 25: Use desk organizers for pens, notepads, and small office supplies
  • [ ] Item 26: Keep a small plant or air purifier to improve workspace air quality
  • [ ] Item 27: Add a whiteboard or bulletin board for notes and reminders
  • [ ] Item 28: Use a filing system or accordion folder for important documents
  • [ ] Item 29: Store extra office supplies in labeled bins or drawers
  • [ ] Item 30: Keep a water bottle or glass at your desk to stay hydrated
  • [ ] Item 31: Test your internet speed and upgrade your plan if below 50 Mbps
  • [ ] Item 32: Use an Ethernet cable for a stable connection during critical tasks
  • [ ] Item 33: Set up a Wi-Fi extender if your signal is weak in your workspace
  • [ ] Item 34: Check your router placement for optimal coverage
  • [ ] Item 35: Run a speed test regularly to monitor internet performance
  • [ ] Item 36: Close unnecessary apps and browser tabs to improve bandwidth usage
  • [ ] Item 37: Use a mesh router system for consistent coverage in larger spaces
  • [ ] Item 38: Position your desk to avoid glare on your screen from windows
  • [ ] Item 39: Use blackout curtains or blinds to control natural light
  • [ ] Item 40: Add a desk lamp with adjustable brightness for task lighting
