72-Point Freelance Contract Review Checklist

Updated: April 01, 2026

A 72-point checklist to review freelance contracts before signing, ensuring all critical terms are included and protecting your business.

What's Inside

  • 72 essential review points
  • covers legal, payment, and scope terms
  • prevents common freelance disputes
  • [ ] Item 1: Verify the contract includes both parties' full legal names and contact information (yours and the client’s).
  • [ ] Item 2: Confirm the contract specifies the exact start date and expected end date of the project.
  • [ ] Item 3: Check that the scope of work is clearly defined in writing, not just verbally referenced.
  • [ ] Item 4: Ensure the scope explicitly lists all services you will provide under the agreement.
  • [ ] Item 5: Confirm the scope excludes any services not agreed upon to avoid scope creep.
  • [ ] Item 6: Verify that any out-of-scope requests will require a separate agreement or fee adjustment.
  • [ ] Item 7: Check that the contract includes a detailed description of the final deliverables (e.g., design files, code, written content).
  • [ ] Item 8: Confirm deliverables are described in measurable terms (e.g., “a fully responsive website with 5 pages”).
  • [ ] Item 9: Ensure deliverables include file formats, versions, and naming conventions where applicable.
  • [ ] Item 10: Check that the client acknowledges receipt of deliverables in writing upon completion.
  • [ ] Item 11: Verify that payment terms specify the total project fee or hourly rate clearly.
  • [ ] Item 12: Confirm whether the fee is fixed, hourly, or milestone-based.
  • [ ] Item 13: Check for a payment schedule with due dates (e.g., 50% upfront, 50% on delivery).
  • [ ] Item 14: Ensure late payment penalties or interest charges are defined, if applicable.
  • [ ] Item 15: Confirm the accepted payment methods (e.g., PayPal, bank transfer, credit card).
  • [ ] Item 16: Verify that expenses (e.g., software, travel) are either included or require prior approval.
  • [ ] Item 17: Check that reimbursable expenses are itemized and require receipts.
  • [ ] Item 18: Confirm the contract outlines how revisions will be handled (e.g., number of rounds, type of changes covered).
  • [ ] Item 19: Ensure revisions are defined as changes within scope, not out-of-scope requests.
  • [ ] Item 20: Check that additional revisions beyond the agreed-upon number incur extra fees.
  • [ ] Item 21: Verify the contract specifies who approves revisions and the approval process.
  • [ ] Item 22: Confirm the timeline includes key milestones with deadlines for client feedback.
  • [ ] Item 23: Check that milestone deadlines are realistic and include buffer time for client review.
  • [ ] Item 24: Ensure the contract includes a final delivery deadline with no open-ended timelines.
  • [ ] Item 25: Confirm the client must provide feedback within a specified timeframe (e.g., 5 business days).
  • [ ] Item 26: Check that delays caused by the client’s late feedback do not extend the project timeline unfairly.
  • [ ] Item 27: Verify the contract defines how communication will occur (e.g., email, Slack, scheduled calls).
  • [ ] Item 28: Confirm response time expectations for client communications (e.g., within 24 hours).
  • [ ] Item 29: Check that urgent requests are clearly defined and compensated separately if needed.
  • [ ] Item 30: Ensure the contract includes a primary point of contact for both parties.
  • [ ] Item 31: Verify the contract states that all project-related communications should be documented.
  • [ ] Item 32: Confirm the confidentiality clause covers all project-related information, not just final deliverables.
  • [ ] Item 33: Check that the NDA (if separate) aligns with the confidentiality terms in the contract.
  • [ ] Item 34: Ensure you are not prohibited from working with similar clients or industries.
  • [ ] Item 35: Verify the duration of confidentiality obligations (e.g., 2 years post-project).
  • [ ] Item 36: Check that proprietary tools, processes, or templates you use are protected from client ownership claims.
  • [ ] Item 37: Confirm the contract assigns ownership of final deliverables to the client only upon full payment.
  • [ ] Item 38: Ensure you retain rights to use project elements (e.g., code snippets, design patterns) in future work unless prohibited.
  • [ ] Item 39: Check that the contract includes a clause allowing you to display the work in your portfolio with client permission.
  • [ ] Item 40: Verify the client cannot claim exclusive rights to similar future projects without your consent.
  • [ ] Item 41: Confirm the termination clause specifies conditions under which either party can end the contract.
  • [ ] Item 42: Check that you can terminate for non-payment after a grace period (e.g., 14 days).
  • [ ] Item 43: Ensure the client can terminate with or without cause, but you’re compensated for work completed.
  • [ ] Item 44: Verify the process for transitioning work if the contract is terminated early.
  • [ ] Item 45: Check that unpaid work is not handed over in case of termination for non-payment.
  • [ ] Item 46: Confirm the liability clause limits your liability to the total fees paid by the client.
  • [ ] Item 47: Ensure you are not held liable for indirect damages (e.g., lost profits, reputational harm).
  • [ ] Item 48: Check that the indemnification clause does not require you to cover the client’s legal costs due to their actions.
  • [ ] Item 49: Verify the force majeure clause includes events like natural disasters, pandemics, or internet outages.
  • [ ] Item 50: Confirm the force majeure clause specifies that timelines are extended, not canceled, in such events.
  • [ ] Item 51: Confirm the contract specifies the scope of work in clear, measurable terms (e.g., deliverables, features, or milestones) to avoid scope creep.
  • [ ] Item 52: Verify that the contract includes a detailed timeline with key deadlines for deliverables and client feedback.
  • [ ] Item 53: Ensure the payment schedule is explicitly outlined, including amounts, due dates, and acceptable payment methods (e.g., bank transfer, PayPal, credit card).
  • [ ] Item 54: Check that the contract specifies the total project cost, including taxes, fees, or additional costs (e.g., software licenses, third-party services).
  • [ ] Item 55: Confirm whether the contract includes a late fee policy for overdue payments and the interest rate or flat fee applied.
  • [ ] Item 56: Verify that the contract defines the cancellation policy, including notice periods, refund terms, and any non-refundable deposits.
  • [ ] Item 57: Ensure the contract outlines the process for requesting changes (e.g., scope adjustments, revisions) and how additional costs will be handled.
  • [ ] Item 58: Check that the contract specifies who owns the intellectual property (IP) rights to the work upon completion and whether partial payments transfer partial rights.
  • [ ] Item 59: Confirm that the contract includes a clause for what happens if the client requests work beyond the original scope (e.g., separate agreement, additional fees).
  • [ ] Item 60: Verify that the contract defines the confidentiality obligations for both parties, including how sensitive information will be handled and protected.
  • [ ] Item 61: Ensure the contract includes a force majeure clause to address unforeseen events (e.g., natural disasters, pandemics) that may delay or prevent project completion.
  • [ ] Item 62: Check that the contract specifies the governing law and jurisdiction in case of disputes (e.g., "This contract shall be governed by the laws of [State/Country]").
  • [ ] Item 63: Verify that the contract includes a dispute resolution process (e.g., mediation, arbitration) before pursuing legal action.
  • [ ] Item 64: Confirm that the contract outlines the process for terminating the agreement, including notice requirements and any penalties or fees.
  • [ ] Item 65: Ensure the contract includes a clause about indemnification, specifying liability for breaches of contract, IP infringement, or damages caused by either party.
  • [ ] Item 66: Check that the contract defines the communication channels and frequency for updates (e.g., weekly emails, Slack, project management tools like Asana or Trello).
  • [ ] Item 67: Verify that the contract includes a clause about client responsibilities, such as providing necessary assets (e.g., logos, content, access credentials) on time.
  • [ ] Item 68: Confirm that the contract specifies the revision policy, including the number of included revisions and how additional revisions will be billed.
  • [ ] Item 69: Ensure the contract includes a clause about subcontracting or outsourcing work, including whether prior approval from the client is required.
  • [ ] Item 70: Check that the contract defines the warranty period for the delivered work (e.g., 30 days post-delivery for bug fixes or adjustments).
  • [ ] Item 71: Verify that the contract includes a clause about data security and compliance, especially if handling sensitive client data (e.g., GDPR, HIPAA).
  • [ ] Item 72: Confirm that both parties have signed and dated the contract, and that each party retains a copy for their records.
