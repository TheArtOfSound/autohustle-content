Template 1: Welcome Email

When to use: Send immediately after a client signs the contract to set expectations and provide next steps.

Subject: Welcome to [Your Service] – Next Steps

Hi [Client's Name],

Thank you for choosing [Your Business Name]! I’m thrilled to work with you on [Project Name].

Here’s what happens next:

  1. Payment: Please complete the payment of [Amount] via [Payment Method]. Your invoice is attached for reference.
  2. Kickoff Call: Schedule our first call here to discuss project details.
  3. Project Access: You’ll receive an email with access to [Project Management Tool, e.g., Trello/Asana] shortly.

What I Need from You:

  • [Document/Info, e.g., Brand Guidelines, Login Details]
  • Your preferred communication method (email/Slack/etc.)

Let me know if you have any questions—I’m happy to help!

Looking forward to collaborating.

Best,

Template 5: Project Kickoff Call Agenda

When to use: Shared before the kickoff call to align expectations and ensure the meeting stays focused.

Subject: Project Kickoff Call Agenda – [Project Name]

Hi [Client's Name],

To make the most of our time together, I’ve outlined the agenda for our kickoff call on [Date/Time]. Please review and let me know if you’d like to add or adjust anything.

Agenda:

  1. Introductions (5 min)
  • Quick intro from both sides (roles, background).
  1. Project Overview (10 min)
  • Recap of project goals and objectives.
  • Key deliverables: [List 3-5 main outputs].
  1. Roles & Responsibilities (10 min)
  • What I’ll handle: [List tasks].
  • What you’ll handle: [List tasks, e.g., "Provide feedback by X date"].
  • Who is the main point of contact on your end? [Name/Title].
  1. Timeline & Milestones (10 min)
  • Review of the project timeline (shared in the welcome email).
  • Dependencies: [List any items that could delay progress].
  1. Communication Plan (10 min)
  • Preferred communication channels: [Email/Slack/Phone].
  • Response times: [e.g., "I’ll reply to emails within 24 hours"].
  • Meeting cadence: [e.g., "Weekly check-ins every Tuesday at 2 PM"].
  1. Q&A + Next Steps (15 min)
  • Open floor for questions.
  • Action items for both sides:
  • You: [Task, e.g., "Share brand assets by EOD Friday"].
  • Me: [Task, e.g., "Draft initial wireframes by next Tuesday"].

Pre-Call Prep:

Please come prepared with:

  • Any questions about the project scope.
  • Brand guidelines or examples of work you love.
  • Access to any tools/platforms we’ll be using (e.g., [Tool Name]).

Can’t wait to dive in! Let me know if you’d like to add anything to the agenda.

Best,

[Your Full Name]

I’ve completed the first draft of [Deliverable Name, e.g., "the homepage design"] for [Project Name]. Please take a look and let me know your thoughts!

What’s Included:

  • [File/Link to Deliverable, e.g., "Figma design file: [Link]"]
  • [Any additional files, e.g., "Mobile mockups: [Link]"]

How to Provide Feedback:

  1. Annotate the File: Use the comment tool in [Tool Name, e.g., "Figma"] to leave specific feedback directly on the designs.
  2. Email or Slack: Alternatively, you can reply to this email or message me in [Slack Channel] with your notes.
  3. Priority Feedback: Please focus on:
  • High-level thoughts (e.g., "This doesn’t align with our brand voice").
  • Structural changes (e.g., "Move the call-to-action button higher").
  • Any content edits needed.

Next Steps:

  • I’ll need your feedback by [Deadline, e.g., "Friday, June 7"] to keep the project on track.
  • If no feedback is received by the deadline, I’ll assume the draft is approved as-is.

Questions? If anything is unclear or you’d like to discuss the draft further, let’s schedule a quick call [Calendar Link].

Looking forward to your thoughts!

Best,

  • Budget: [Original budget].
