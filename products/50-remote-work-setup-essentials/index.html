50 Remote Work Setup Essentials

Updated: April 01, 2026

50-step checklist for setting up a productive remote workspace — includes hardware, software, and ergonomic checks.

What's Inside

  • 50 essential setup items
  • covers hardware, software, and ergonomics
  • step-by-step checklist format
  • [ ] Item 1: Position your desk in a quiet corner away from high-traffic areas to minimize distractions
  • [ ] Item 2: Choose a desk with at least 30 inches of depth to accommodate your monitor and essentials
  • [ ] Item 3: Ensure your chair has adjustable height settings to maintain a 90-degree angle at your knees
  • [ ] Item 4: Adjust the backrest to support the natural curve of your lower back
  • [ ] Item 5: Set the seat depth so that there’s a small gap (2–3 inches) between the edge and the back of your knees
  • [ ] Item 6: Position your feet flat on the floor or use a footrest if needed to keep hips and knees level
  • [ ] Item 7: Install an anti-fatigue mat to reduce strain during prolonged standing if using a sit-stand desk
  • [ ] Item 8: Calibrate your monitor so the top third of the screen is at or slightly below eye level
  • [ ] Item 9: Place the monitor at arm’s length (about 20–30 inches) from your eyes
  • [ ] Item 10: Use a monitor stand or stack sturdy books to elevate a laptop screen to eye level
  • [ ] Item 11: Connect an external keyboard and mouse if using a laptop in clamshell mode
  • [ ] Item 12: Position your keyboard so your wrists are straight and not bent upward or downward
  • [ ] Item 13: Use a wrist rest only if it supports neutral wrist alignment without pressure on nerves
  • [ ] Item 14: Choose an ergonomic mouse that fits your hand size and grip style to prevent strain
  • [ ] Item 15: Set up a laptop cooling pad if your device runs warm during extended use
  • [ ] Item 16: Ensure your internet router is within range or upgrade to a mesh Wi-Fi system for reliable connectivity
  • [ ] Item 17: Use a wired Ethernet cable for primary internet connection if possible, especially for video calls
  • [ ] Item 18: Upgrade your internet plan to at least 50 Mbps download and 10 Mbps upload for smooth video conferencing
  • [ ] Item 19: Install a business-grade VPN to encrypt your internet traffic when accessing sensitive files
  • [ ] Item 20: Use a wired headset with a built-in microphone for clearer audio on calls
  • [ ] Item 21: Invest in a webcam with 1080p resolution and autofocus for professional video quality
  • [ ] Item 22: Arrange dual monitors side-by-side at the same height to reduce neck movement
  • [ ] Item 23: Use a docking station to simplify connectivity between multiple devices and peripherals
  • [ ] Item 24: Set up a second monitor for reference materials and keep primary tasks on the main screen
  • [ ] Item 25: Install task lighting (e.g., LED desk lamp) to avoid glare on your screen
  • [ ] Item 26: Position your desk perpendicular to windows to reduce glare and screen reflections
  • [ ] Item 27: Use blackout curtains to control natural light and reduce screen glare during peak hours
  • [ ] Item 28: Choose a color temperature of 4000K–5000K for task lighting to reduce eye strain
  • [ ] Item 29: Add a small LED strip under the desk for ambient lighting without glare
  • [ ] Item 30: Use a glare filter or matte screen protector if your monitor reflects too much light
  • [ ] Item 31: Organize cables with clips, sleeves, or a cable management box to reduce clutter
  • [ ] Item 32: Use desk trays or drawers to store stationery, notebooks, and small tech accessories
  • [ ] Item 33: Assign a labeled folder or bin for incoming physical mail and documents
  • [ ] Item 34: Keep frequently used items (notebook, pens, chargers) within arm’s reach
  • [ ] Item 35: Use vertical file organizers on the wall to save desk space and keep files accessible
  • [ ] Item 36: Take a 5-minute break every 30 minutes to stretch your wrists, neck, and shoulders
  • [ ] Item 37: Perform eye exercises (e.g., 20-20-20 rule: every 20 minutes, look 20 feet away for 20 seconds)
  • [ ] Item 38: Drink water from a large, spill-proof bottle to stay hydrated throughout the day
  • [ ] Item 39: Use a standing desk converter if you want to alternate between sitting and standing
  • [ ] Item 40: Set reminders to stand up and walk around every hour to improve circulation
  • [ ] Item 41: Install a habit-tracking app or calendar reminder to log breaks and movement
  • [ ] Item 42: Use noise-canceling headphones to block out household or street noise during deep work
  • [ ] Item 43: Integrate a white noise app or smart speaker to mask background distractions
  • [ ] Item 44: Use a password manager to store and generate strong, unique passwords for work accounts
  • [ ] Item 45: Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) on all work-related accounts and tools
  • [ ] Item 46: Set up automatic device and cloud backups for critical files and projects
  • [ ] Item 47: Use an external hard drive or NAS for offline backups of large files (e.g., videos, datasets)
  • [ ] Item 48: Schedule weekly or monthly maintenance to update software, delete old files, and run antivirus scans
  • [ ] Item 49: Personalize your workspace with a plant, photo frame, or motivational poster to boost morale
  • [ ] Item 50: Conduct a weekly review of your setup to identify ergonomic or efficiency improvements
