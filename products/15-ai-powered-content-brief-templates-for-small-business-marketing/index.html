  3. Section 2: [Title + 2-3 key points; address another pain point]
  4. Section 3: [Title + 2-3 key points; include data or examples]
  5. Section 4: [Title + 2-3 key points; address remaining pain point]

  • Use [Secondary Keywords] naturally in 1-2 sections.
  • Aim for a readability score of [e.g., 80+ on Hemingway Editor].
