  1. "Generate a 12-month cash flow forecast for our retail business, incorporating seasonal sales patterns, inventory turnover rates, and expected supplier payment terms. Highlight months with potential cash shortfalls and recommend strategies to mitigate liquidity risks."
  1. "Compare the total cost of ownership (TCO) of leasing versus purchasing a fleet of 50 electric vehicles for our logistics operations over a 5-year period. Include factors such as depreciation, maintenance, energy costs, and potential tax incentives in your analysis."

10. "Assess the financial impact of implementing AI-powered fraud detection in our accounts payable process. Calculate expected savings from reduced fraudulent payments, implementation costs, and projected ROI over three years, including training and maintenance expenses."

11. "Generate a prompt that automates the creation of quarterly board reports by consolidating financial KPIs, operational metrics, and strategic initiatives. Ensure the report highlights year-over-year performance and includes visualizations for key trends."

12. "Evaluate the profitability of discontinuing our lowest-performing product line by analyzing contribution margins, allocated overhead costs, and customer lifetime value metrics. Provide a decision matrix that weighs financial outcomes against strategic considerations like brand positioning."

13. "Develop a prompt that identifies and quantifies cost-saving opportunities in our supply chain by analyzing supplier performance, lead times, and bulk purchasing discounts. Prioritize recommendations based on potential savings versus implementation effort."

14. "Create a financial health dashboard prompt that tracks real-time metrics such as burn rate, runway, and customer acquisition cost (CAC) payback period. Set up automated alerts when metrics deviate from target ranges or trigger predefined risk thresholds."

15. "Analyze our customer segmentation data to identify high-value clients with declining engagement. Propose a retention strategy that includes personalized pricing, loyalty incentives, and targeted upsell campaigns, with projected revenue impact over 12 months."

16. "Generate a prompt that automates the reconciliation of intercompany transactions between our global subsidiaries. Ensure it flags discrepancies in currency, timing, or accounting treatment and suggests corrective journal entries to align with group accounting policies."

17. "Assess the financial feasibility of upgrading our ERP system by comparing the total cost of ownership for on-premise versus cloud-based solutions over a 7-year horizon. Include factors such as scalability, integration costs, and compliance requirements in your analysis."

18. "Develop a prompt that performs scenario analysis for our next product launch, modeling best-case, base-case, and worst-case revenue projections based on market adoption rates, pricing elasticity, and competitive responses. Include sensitivity analysis for key variables."

19. "Create a financial compliance monitoring prompt that checks for adherence to IFRS 9, ASC 606, and local GAAP standards in our monthly financial statements. Flag any potential non-compliance issues and suggest corrective actions to mitigate audit risks."

20. "Evaluate the cost-benefit of implementing blockchain technology for our supply chain financing processes. Quantify potential reductions in transaction fees, processing times, and fraud risk, then compare against implementation and maintenance costs."

21. "Generate a prompt that identifies tax optimization opportunities in our cross-border transactions. Analyze transfer pricing structures, withholding tax rates, and double taxation treaties to recommend strategies that minimize global effective tax rate while ensuring compliance."

22. "Develop a financial planning and analysis (FP&A) prompt that automates the creation of rolling 3-year forecasts. Incorporate macroeconomic indicators, industry trends, and company-specific growth drivers to generate dynamic revenue, expense, and cash flow projections."

23. "Assess the financial impact of adopting a hybrid work model on our office space requirements. Model the potential savings from reduced square footage versus the costs of remote work infrastructure, including technology upgrades and cybersecurity measures."

24. "Create a prompt that performs benchmarking analysis of our financial ratios against industry peers. Identify gaps in liquidity, profitability, and efficiency metrics, then recommend corrective actions tailored to our business model and market position."

25. "Generate a prompt that automates the detection of anomalies in our expense reports by analyzing patterns in merchant payments, reimbursement frequencies, and policy violations. Prioritize high-risk transactions for manual review and suggest policy adjustments to prevent future issues."
