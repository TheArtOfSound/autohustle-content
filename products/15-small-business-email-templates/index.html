Template 1: New Client Introduction

When to use: To welcome a new client and outline the next steps in your working relationship.

Subject: Welcome to [Your Business Name], [Client Name]!

Hi [Client Name],

Welcome to [Your Business Name]! We’re thrilled to have you on board and are excited to start working together. This email outlines the next steps to ensure a smooth collaboration.

What’s Next:

  1. Initial Consultation: Let’s schedule a call to discuss your goals and project details. [Book a time here: [Calendly Link] | Propose 2-3 time slots].
  2. Project Kickoff: Once confirmed, we’ll send over a detailed plan and timeline.
  3. Next Steps: You’ll receive a welcome packet with all the information you need to get started.

What We Need From You:

  • Any specific requirements or preferences for the project.
  • Access to necessary tools or platforms (e.g., [list tools if applicable]).
  • Your availability for the consultation call.

We’ve attached our [Welcome Guide/Terms of Service/Contract] for your review. Please sign and return the contract by [deadline] to finalize our agreement.

Let us know if you have any questions—we’re here to help! Looking forward to collaborating with you.

Best regards,

[Your Full Name]

[Your Business Name]

[Your Email Address]

[Your Phone Number]

[Briefly describe the project goals and deliverables.]

  1. [Deliverable 1] – [Description]
  2. [Deliverable 2] – [Description]
  3. [Deliverable 3] – [Description]

Timeline:

  • Start Date: [Date]
  • Milestone 1: [Date]
  • Milestone 2: [Date]
  • Final Delivery: [Date]

Investment:

Our total investment for this project is [Amount] and includes:

  • [List of services or deliverables included]

Please review the proposal and let us know if you have any questions or if you’d like to discuss any adjustments. If this aligns with your vision, we can proceed with signing the contract and starting the project.

We’re excited about the potential to collaborate and bring [Project Name] to life. Looking forward to your feedback!

We’re really excited about the opportunity to work with you and believe this project will [mention a key benefit, e.g., "drive significant growth for your business"]. Please let us know your thoughts at your earliest convenience.
